# Eliud Kipchoge's Official Marathon World Record Pace Calculations

## Official Record Confirmation
- **Time**: 2:01:09 (2 hours, 1 minute, 9 seconds)
- **Date**: September 25, 2022
- **Event**: Berlin Marathon
- **Source**: World Athletics Record Progression Page

## Time Conversion
- Total time in seconds: (2 × 3600) + (1 × 60) + 9 = 7269 seconds

## Distance
- Standard marathon distance: 42.195 km = 42,195 meters

## Pace Calculations

### Per Kilometer
- Seconds per kilometer: 7269 ÷ 42.195 = 172.27 seconds/km
- Minutes per kilometer: 172.27 ÷ 60 = 2.871 minutes/km
- Format as minutes:seconds: 2 minutes and 52.27 seconds per kilometer
- Final pace: **2:52.27 min/km**

### Per Meter
- Seconds per meter: 7269 ÷ 42195 = 0.17227 seconds/meter
- Final pace: **0.17227 sec/m**
