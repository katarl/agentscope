- 1 minute = 1 × 60 = 60 seconds

### Pace per Kilometer
- Total time ÷ Distance = 7269 seconds ÷ 42.195 km
- **Pace per kilometer**: 172.27 seconds/km
- Convert to minutes/seconds: 172.27 ÷ 60 = 2 minutes with remainder 52.27 seconds
- **Final pace per kilometer**: **2 minutes and 52.27 seconds per kilometer (2:52.27/km)**

### Pace per Meter
